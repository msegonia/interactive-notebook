      Class Record

      Araling Panlipunan 8
      Add scores
      For paper quizzes, outputs and exams. Import: a ZipGrade export (.csv, Standard Format) or your Class Record Excel file.
      Show the scores to students
      Show the term grade to students
      Answered, but not in this Class Record yet
      Tap an activity’s name at the top of its column to give it a short name, like Q2.1 or PT1, or to combine it with other quizzes into one column. In a ✎ typed column, tap a box and type the score; press Enter to go to the next student.
      How the grades are computed (same as your E-Class Record): Written Work 20%, Performance Task 50%, Exams 30% (Summative 1 = 30, Summative 2 = 30, Term Exam = 40). A blank score stays blank and counts as 0, like an empty box in Excel. Quizzes appear here when you turn on Put this score in the Class Record and a student of this section has answered.
